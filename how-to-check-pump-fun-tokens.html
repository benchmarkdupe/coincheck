How to check a pump.fun token

Six steps, all of them on-chain, all of them readable in seconds. The order matters, because the first two eliminate most of what goes wrong.

Run a free check How we score

1. Authorities

Live mint authority means supply can be printed into your position. A freeze authority means your account can be frozen. Both are invisible in a price chart.

2. Concentration

If one wallet holds a large share, its exit sets the price for everyone. Top-ten share against the supply actually observed is the figure to look at.

3. Exit depth

What share of supply is inside a pool you could sell into. This is the number that decides whether a bad outcome is survivable.

4. Fresh-wallet clusters

Supply distributed to wallets that did nothing but receive it is the shape of a farmed launch. Suggestive, not proof.

5. Deployer history

A prior trail of failed launches is worth knowing about, and worth discounting heavily, because rotating wallets defeats it.

6. What you could not read

The most important step. A tool that will not tell you what it could not see is asking for trust it has not earned.
