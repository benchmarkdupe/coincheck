The platform question and the token question

Whether the launchpad is trustworthy is separate from whether this particular mint is safe. Tools like CoinCheck answer the second question, because the first is not really in dispute.

What is verifiable on every launch

Who holds the supply, whether the authorities are live, how much of the supply is sellable, and whether the deployer has a history of launches that failed.

What is not verifiable

Whether the people holding supply plan to sell together. The commonest way a memecoin loses money is everyone deciding to exit at once, and that intent is not recorded anywhere on-chain.

The honest position

On-chain checks remove the traps that are written into the contract. They cannot remove the decision by the holder group, and any tool claiming otherwise is selling something.
