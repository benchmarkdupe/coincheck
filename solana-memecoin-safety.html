A useful risk tool has to be honest about the boundary between what it can measure and what it cannot. That boundary is where most losses actually live.

Readable on-chain

Mint and freeze authority, Token-2022 transfer fees and permanent delegate, holder concentration, pool coverage, and the deployer's observed history.

Not readable

Whether the holders intend to sell together. The commonest way a memecoin loses money is a coordinated exit, and intent is not recorded anywhere.

Also not readable

Cross-chain wallet clustering is not implemented. Insider and bundle activity is the hardest pattern to fake and the most valuable to detect, which is why it tops the known-gaps list.

How to use a tool like this

Treat 'no red flags' as 'nothing was found', never as 'safe'. A clean check narrows the field; it does not close it.
